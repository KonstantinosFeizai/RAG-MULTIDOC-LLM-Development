# NovaTech Solutions — Employee Handbook (2026 Edition)

## 1. Company Overview & Core Values

NovaTech Solutions is a mid-sized cloud infrastructure company founded in 2019. Our headquarters is located in Athens, Greece, with regional remote hubs in Berlin and Lisbon.

Our three core operating principles are:

1. **Default to Transparency:** Document decisions publicly in internal wikis before scheduling meetings.
2. **Ship Small, Ship Often:** Production deployments happen every Tuesday and Thursday at 14:00 EET.
3. **Customer Zero:** Every engineer must use our internal staging product, _NovaPulse_, for at least 30 minutes per week.

---

## 2. Work Hours & Remote Work Policy

NovaTech operates on a hybrid-first schedule.

- **Core Collaboration Hours:** All employees, regardless of time zone, must be reachable on Slack between **11:00 and 15:00 EET**.
- **Office Days:** Employees living within 30 km of the Athens HQ are expected to work on-site on **Wednesdays**.
- **Work-From-Anywhere (WFA) Allowance:** Full-time employees may work internationally for up to **25 business days per calendar year**, provided they notify their manager and HR at least 14 days in advance.

---

## 3. Time Off & Leave Policies

- **Annual Paid Vacation:** Every full-time employee receives **24 days** of paid annual leave per year, accruing at 2 days per month. Unused days up to a maximum of 5 days can roll over into Q1 of the following year.
- **Sick Leave:** Employees receive up to **10 paid sick days** per year without requiring a doctor's note. Absences exceeding 3 consecutive days require a medical certificate uploaded to the HR portal.
- **Birthday Leave:** All employees get an extra paid day off during the month of their birthday, known internally as a _"NovaDay"_.
- **Parental Leave:** Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of 100% paid leave; secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of 100% paid leave.

---

## 4. Equipment & Home Office Stipend

- **Laptop Refresh Cycle:** Company laptops (MacBook Pro M4 or ThinkPad X1 Carbon) are replaced every **3 years**.
- **Home Office Budget:** New hires receive a one-time **$750 USD** (or €700 EUR) stipend to purchase a monitor, ergonomic chair, or desk. Receipts must be submitted via Expensify within 60 days of the start date.
- **Learning & Development Stipend:** Each employee has an annual budget of **$1,200 USD** for books, courses, certifications, or tech conferences. Unused funds expire on December 15th.

---

## 5. Expense & Travel Reimbursement

- **Meal Per Diem (Business Travel):** Up to **€65 EUR per day** for meals when traveling for client visits or conferences. Alcohol is strictly excluded from reimbursement.
- **Flight Policy:** Economy class is mandatory for all flights under **6 hours**. Premium Economy may be booked for flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval.
- **Approval Threshold:** Any single expense exceeding **€250 EUR** requires pre-approval from a Department Head before purchase.

---

## 6. IT Security & AI Usage Guidelines

- **Password Manager:** All work credentials must be stored in **1Password**. Sharing passwords via Slack or email is strictly prohibited.
- **Approved AI Tools:** Employees may use company-hosted local LLMs or the enterprise tier of approved AI coding assistants. Pasting proprietary source code, customer API keys, or PII (Personally Identifiable Information) into public, free-tier web chatbots is a Class-1 security violation.
- **Incident Reporting:** Suspected phishing emails or lost devices must be reported immediately to `security@novatech-internal.example` or via the `#sec-alerts` Slack channel within **2 hours** of discovery.
